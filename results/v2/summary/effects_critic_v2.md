# Factorial effects with intervals and multiple-comparison correction (critic_v2 runs)

Effects on log test MAPE of the 2x2x2 design, per dataset. `change_pct` is the average change in MAPE from switching the part on (negative is better), with a 95% interval from the seed-to-seed spread. `p` is a t-test over seeds. `p_holm_dataset` is corrected for the 7 effects tested on that dataset, `p_holm_main` for the main effects of all datasets together. `p_wilcoxon` is given only with 6 or more seeds: with 5 its smallest possible value is 0.0625, so it cannot reach 0.05. Sources: `critic_v2` seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5. Runs without a finite test MAPE: 0. Generated by `python -m cwp.analysis effects --exp critic_v2`.

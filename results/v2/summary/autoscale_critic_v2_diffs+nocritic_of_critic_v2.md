# Provisioning cost of the forecasts (critic_v2_diffs+nocritic_of_critic_v2 runs)

One VM per forecast job on the test part, mean ± standard deviation over seeds. `under_rate`: % of intervals with too few VMs. `missing_vm_pct` / `idle_vm_pct`: VMs short / unused, as % of all jobs. `headroom_pct_for_T`: the smallest fixed extra capacity (in steps of 1%) on top of the forecast that keeps the share of short intervals at or under T%, and `idle_vm_pct_at_T` the unused VMs it costs. The headroom is read off the test part, so it describes each forecast's trade-off curve; it is not a tuned policy. Generated by `python -m cwp.analysis autoscale --exp critic_v2_diffs --nocritic-from critic_v2`.
